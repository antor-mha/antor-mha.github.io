Academic projects, described as they stand today.

Intelligent phishing email detector

Final semester project · in progress

A user pastes an email and an NLP model labels it Phishing or Legitimate. The focus is a practical web app with explainable results, such as urgent language, requests for sensitive information and suspicious calls to action, while comparing suitable models. Planned extensions: link-based, spear and image-based phishing.

Tools
NLP, text classification, web app
Status
Prototype under development

Chicken disease detection

Academic IoT and ML project

A camera mounted on a small car moves through a poultry area and a machine learning model looks for signs of disease in what it sees.

Tools

Status
Choosing the model architecture

Supply chain analytics and decision support

Academic project · Dept. of CIS, Daffodil International University

Organizations often keep supply chain data in many Excel files, which slows decisions on inventory, suppliers and deliveries. This project cleans and combines the data with Power Query, models it as a star schema, and presents KPIs, risk analysis and what-if scenarios in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Tools
Power BI, Power Query, star schema
